About Me

Lago Brian

I'm a Financial Engineer, Data Scientist, and writer based in Nairobi, Kenya. My work sits at the intersection of quantitative finance, machine learning, and algorithmic trading - building tools and writing research that both have to hold up when the market is open.

Before finance, I spent 2012 to 2013 as a healthcare assistant at the Aga Khan Hospital in Nairobi. Surgical ward. Post-op patients - gunshot wounds, stroke survivors, people who had just come through something serious. You learn quickly that process matters, that staying calm when something is going wrong is a skill you build deliberately, not a personality trait you're born with. I still think about that.
